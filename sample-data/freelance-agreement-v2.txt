FREELANCE SERVICE AGREEMENT (VERSION B)

1. PARTIES
This Freelance Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between BrightPath Digital Pvt. Ltd. ("Client") and the undersigned independent contractor ("Freelancer"), collectively the "Parties."

2. SCOPE OF WORK
Freelancer agrees to design and develop a responsive marketing website for Client, including up to eight (8) pages, two (2) rounds of revisions per page, and full on-page SEO setup, as further described in Exhibit A.

3. PAYMENT
Client shall pay Freelancer a total fee of ₹25,000, payable as follows: 50% (₹12,500) as an advance upon signing, and the remaining 50% (₹12,500) within seven (7) days of final delivery. Late payments beyond fifteen (15) days shall accrue interest at 2% per month on the outstanding balance.

4. TERM
This Agreement begins on the date of signing and continues until the completion of the Scope of Work, unless earlier terminated as provided herein, and thereafter as described in Section 11.

5. TERMINATION
Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing sixty (60) days' written notice to the other Party. If Client terminates without cause after work has begun, Client shall pay Freelancer for all work completed up to the date of termination, plus a 10% early termination fee on the remaining contract value.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Freelancer agrees to keep confidential all non-public business, technical, and financial information disclosed by Client during the engagement, and shall not disclose such information to any third party during the term of this Agreement and for a period of three (3) years following its termination.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Upon receipt of full payment, all intellectual property rights in the final deliverables shall transfer to Client. Freelancer waives any right to display the work in Freelancer's professional portfolio without Client's prior written consent.

8. LIABILITY
Freelancer's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed two (2) times the total fees actually paid by Client under this Agreement.

9. INDEMNIFICATION
Freelancer agrees to indemnify and hold Client harmless from any third-party claims arising from Freelancer's performance of the services under this Agreement, including claims arising from ordinary negligence.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Bengaluru, India.

11. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
For ongoing maintenance services beyond the initial project, this Agreement shall automatically renew on a month-to-month basis unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least fifteen (15) days before the end of the then-current term.

12. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
